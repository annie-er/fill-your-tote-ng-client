Fill your tote! offers sustainable & artsy tote bags, designed just for you

Who We Are

Fill Your Tote! is a one-woman shop run by Annie, a Computer Science student at the University of Toronto. What started as a passion project became a small business in June 2023 through Summer Company. Annie enjoys creating visual stories on tote bags for people to wear. Her drawings revolve around friendship, human emotion, and love.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to connect our community with thoughtfully crafted, eco-friendly tote bags while fostering meaningful relationships between clients and creators. Fill Your Tote! strives to offer compelling, artsy tote bags that resonate with the people who carry them (no more boring totes).

How Things Work

Step 1. Discuss

Tell us your vision and we'll bring it to life though a compelling design on an eco-friendly canvas tote.

Step 2. Design

After providing a few sample designs, you pick a final design that we handcraft onto your totes.

Step 3. Deliver

Your one-of-a-kind tote is carefully packaged and delivered to your door. You can finally start wearing them!
